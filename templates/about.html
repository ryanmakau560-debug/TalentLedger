About TalentLedger

TalentLedger was created to bring accountability and clear value to peer-to-peer skill sharing. Knowledge should not be traded without a fair, standardized measure of time and contribution.

Our Solution: The Time-Bank

TalentLedger runs on a Time-Bank model where teaching and learning are tracked through a transparent ledger. Live mentoring and teaching sessions happen over Discord calls so learners and educators can connect directly, while Django tracks credits, bookings, and session records reliably in the background.

How It Works

  • Earn 1 Skill Credit per hour: Teaching a 1-hour session earns the educator 1 Skill Credit.
  • Redeem credits to learn: Spend earned credits—or starting credits—to book sessions with other members.
  • Session feedback & accountability: Learners rate sessions to keep teaching quality high and verifiable.

Every booking, credit transfer, and session status is recorded so your learning history and teaching contributions stay clear and verifiable as the platform grows.
