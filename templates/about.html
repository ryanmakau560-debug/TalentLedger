About TalentLedger

TalentLedger was founded to solve the lack of accountability in common skill exchange platforms[cite: 2]. We believe that knowledge should not be traded without a standardized measure of value[cite: 2].

Our Solution: The Time-Bank

We have developed a robust Time-Bank platform where learning is tracked and valued[cite: 2]. In our system, teaching takes place via Discord calls, ensuring a personal and direct learning experience[cite: 2].

How It Works

  • After a session, the recipient rates the teacher to ensure quality[cite: 2].
  • Each hour taught earns the educator exactly 1 'Skill Credit'[cite: 2].
  • These credits can be seamlessly redeemed to learn new skills from others on the platform[cite: 2].

By integrating a dedicated ledger system for tracking contributions, we make learning progress transparent and verifiable[cite: 2]. TalentLedger is designed to be scalable and is built with a secure Django backend to support your growth[cite: 2].
