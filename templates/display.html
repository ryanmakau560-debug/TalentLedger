WHO WE ARE

Accountable Skill Exchange Built for Real Growth

Many skill-swap spaces lack structure—people trade tips informally, sessions get missed, and there is no reliable way to measure what you contribute versus what you receive.

TalentLedger gives members a transparent Time-Bank system where live Discord mentoring pairs naturally with verifiable session tracking and Skill Credits.

Why Members Use TalentLedger

Whether you are teaching a coding concept, walking through design feedback, or booking help from someone else, the workflow stays clear from start to finish:

1. Request & Confirm: Learners send booking requests and instructors accept or decline them.

2. Meet on Discord: Connect directly in live calls for hands-on guidance.

3. Rate & Earn Credits: Learners rate the session and instructors earn Skill Credits.

THE TIME-BANK IDEA

Value Beyond Cash

Your time and expertise matter even when you are a student or building your portfolio. Teaching one person builds credits you can spend learning from someone completely different across the platform.

Instead of keeping score in your head, the ledger keeps track of your bookings, hours, and credits cleanly.
