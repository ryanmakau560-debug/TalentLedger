THE IDEA

Accountable skill exchange built around real time and live mentoring.

1 Hour = 1 Skill Credit

Teaching an hour earns 1 Skill Credit in your ledger. Redeem your credits to book sessions with other members whenever you want to learn something new.

Live Sessions on Discord

Once a booking is accepted, connect directly over Discord calls for real-time walkthroughs, feedback, and hands-on help.

Ratings & Clear Records

Learners rate sessions after completion while bookings and credit balances stay tracked cleanly on a Django backend.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple flow from booking request to earned credits.

01 / DISCOVER

Browse or Post a Skill

Explore skills shared by the community or add your own so learners can request sessions with you.

02 / SESSION

Confirm & Meet on Discord

Instructors review incoming requests and accept or decline them, then meet live over Discord.

03 / LEDGER

Rate & Grow Your Credits

Leave a rating after the session and watch your Skill Credits and activity update on your dashboard.
