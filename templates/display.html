HOW IT WORKS

Simple Flow from Request to Credit

Every session follows a clear three-step loop so both learners and educators know where things stand.

01 / REQUEST

Book or Offer a Skill

List skills you can teach or send a booking request to an instructor for the topic you want to learn.

02 / SESSION

Meet Live on Discord

Once approved, connect directly over a Discord call for live mentoring, walkthroughs, and Q&A.

03 / CREDITS

Rate & Build Credits

Learners rate the session for accountability while teaching hours add up into redeemable Skill Credits.
